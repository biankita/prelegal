# Prelegal — App Use Cases

Prelegal is an AI-powered legal document drafting tool that lets you describe what you need in plain language and generates a complete, ready-to-sign agreement. The following use cases illustrate who benefits and how.

---

## 1. Startup Exploring a Partnership (Mutual NDA)

**Who:** Two early-stage founders who want to explore a potential collaboration or acquisition conversation.

**Scenario:** Before sharing pitch decks, financials, or product roadmaps, both sides need a confidentiality agreement in place. Neither party has in-house counsel.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user types something like "I need an NDA before sharing our product plans with a potential acquirer." The AI asks for the party names, governing state, and the purpose of sharing, then generates a signed-ready Mutual NDA — in minutes instead of days.

---

## 2. SaaS Company Onboarding a New Customer (Cloud Service Agreement)

**Who:** A B2B software provider signing up its first paying enterprise customer.

**Scenario:** The provider needs a standard subscription contract covering service access, support tiers, payment terms, acceptable use, and data handling before the customer goes live.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user describes the service, subscription period, pricing, and support level. The AI drafts a Cloud Service Agreement (CSA) with all key terms filled in, eliminating the need to hire outside counsel for a boilerplate deal.

---

## 3. Product Team Recruiting Beta Testers (Design Partner Agreement)

**Who:** An early-stage product team that wants structured, committed beta users in exchange for early access.

**Scenario:** The team is pre-launch and needs to formalize relationships with five design partners — capturing their feedback obligations, the product access grant, IP ownership of feedback, and confidentiality.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user explains the program and the AI collects partner details, program duration, fees (if any), and feedback cadence, then generates a Design Partner Agreement per partner.

---

## 4. Software Vendor Running a Trial (Pilot Agreement)

**Who:** A mid-market software company offering a 90-day paid pilot to a prospective enterprise customer.

**Scenario:** The vendor wants the trial governed by a lightweight agreement that sets scope, fees, success criteria, and what happens at the end of the pilot period.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user describes the pilot scope and timeline. The AI asks targeted questions and produces a Pilot Agreement — giving both parties clear expectations without a full contract negotiation.

---

## 5. Agency Scoping a Client Engagement (Professional Services Agreement)

**Who:** A consulting firm or digital agency starting a new client project.

**Scenario:** Before kicking off, the agency needs a services contract that covers deliverables, timelines, payment milestones, IP assignment, and liability limits.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user describes the project and the AI drafts a Professional Services Agreement (PSA) capturing the agreed scope, fees, and terms — protecting both the agency and the client.

---

## 6. Infrastructure Provider Defining Uptime Commitments (SLA)

**Who:** A managed services or cloud infrastructure company promising customers specific reliability levels.

**Scenario:** The provider offers tiered SLAs (99.9% vs 99.99% uptime) and needs a standard document to attach to each customer contract.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user specifies uptime targets, measurement windows, exclusions, and remedies. The AI generates a Service Level Agreement ready to attach as an exhibit to the main CSA.

---

## 7. Tech Company Structuring a Go-to-Market Alliance (Partnership Agreement)

**Who:** Two technology companies creating a reseller, referral, or co-sell arrangement.

**Scenario:** The companies need to define revenue sharing, marketing obligations, territory restrictions, IP usage, and term before launching a joint go-to-market program.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user describes the nature of the partnership and the AI asks about revenue splits, exclusivity, and term, then produces a Partnership Agreement both sides can review and sign.

---

## 8. ISV Licensing Its Software (Software License Agreement)

**Who:** An independent software vendor (ISV) distributing its software to enterprise customers under a license model (not SaaS).

**Scenario:** The vendor needs to define license scope, restrictions, fees, support, and IP ownership for on-premise or downloaded software.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user specifies the software, license type (per seat, site, etc.), fees, and support terms. The AI generates a Software License Agreement tailored to the deal.

---

## 9. Company Processing EU Customer Data (Data Processing Agreement)

**Who:** Any company that handles personal data on behalf of its customers and is subject to GDPR or similar regulations.

**Scenario:** An enterprise customer is requiring a DPA before they can sign the main service contract, and the vendor's legal team is backed up.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user provides the parties' roles (controller/processor), categories of data, processing purposes, and security measures. The AI generates a GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreement quickly.

---

## 10. Healthcare Tech Company Working with a Covered Entity (BAA)

**Who:** A health-tech SaaS vendor whose product touches Protected Health Information (PHI).

**Scenario:** Before integrating with a hospital or health plan, the vendor must execute a Business Associate Agreement covering PHI handling, breach notification, and permitted uses under HIPAA.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user describes the services and the categories of PHI involved. The AI generates a Business Associate Agreement that satisfies HIPAA requirements — critical for vendors who cannot afford compliance missteps.

---

## 11. Enterprise Adding AI Tools to an Existing Vendor Relationship (AI Addendum)

**Who:** A legal or IT team at a company whose existing vendors have started incorporating AI features.

**Scenario:** The company's InfoSec and legal teams need to add explicit terms around AI feature use — covering data inputs, model training restrictions, output accuracy disclaimers, and compliance — as an addendum to an existing agreement.

**How Prelegal helps:** The user specifies the existing agreement, AI features involved, and the restrictions required. The AI drafts an AI Addendum that attaches cleanly to the existing contract.

---

## Common Thread

Across all of these scenarios, Prelegal removes the bottleneck of drafting from scratch or waiting on outside counsel for standard agreements. Users describe their situation in plain language, the AI asks clarifying questions, and the output is a fully populated, downloadable document — ready for review and signature.
